# Holistic interface review

Resolve the exact screen or flow and choose a mode:

- **Quick:** inspect the primary path across all domains; report at most five
  high- or medium-impact findings.
- **Full:** inspect accessibility, layout, writing, typography, color, and
  polish in that order, including loading, empty, error, and narrow states when
  present; report at most fifteen findings.

Inspect one domain at a time and keep reduced findings before loading the next
domain's references. Consolidate repeated symptoms under one root cause.
Require source or rendered evidence for every finding. Rank by user impact,
reach, and leverage.

A review request is read-only unless implementation was also requested. Report
scope and coverage, ranked findings, plausible candidates rejected for lack of
evidence or benefit, verification performed, and one verdict: Block, Needs
changes, or Approve.
