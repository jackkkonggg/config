# Interface writing

Inspect nearby copy, product terminology, localization conventions, and the
stakes before editing.

- Prefer brief, plain language and consistent terms over novelty.
- Keep one product voice while adjusting tone to context. Errors and
  destructive actions should be calm, direct, and explicit.
- Use verb-first action labels. Consequential confirmations repeat the action.
- Link text describes its destination; avoid device-specific "click here."
- Label toggles for their enabled state.
- Put actionable errors near the failing input and explain recovery.
- Empty states orient the reader and offer one useful next action.
- Placeholders show format; they never replace visible labels.
- Use complete localizable strings rather than concatenated fragments.

Preserve intentional brand character unless it creates ambiguity,
inconsistency, translation risk, or an inappropriate tone.
